# Activity Photos Directory

Drop photos of your real-world activities, team events, robotics lab work, and competitions here.

### Recommended Files:
- `krsri-robotics.jpg` — Photo of the KRSRI fire-fighting robot chassis, debugging in the lab, or team testing.
- `brawijaya-lab.jpg` — Photo of your electrical engineering lab workbench, circuit breadboards, or oscilloscope work.
- `leadership-council.jpg` — Photo of your student council administration or school activity.
- `prime-competition.jpg` — Photo of the PRIME competition presentation, banner, or semifinalist team.
- `agrocomplex-award.jpg` — Photo of the 1st place scientific design competition award or prototype showcase.

### Recommended Specs:
- **Format**: JPG, PNG, or WebP
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 or 4:3
- **Size**: Ideally under 500KB for fast loading

Once you add an image here, update the `image` field for that experience or credential item in `app/portfolio.tsx`:
```ts
image: "/activities/krsri-robotics.jpg",
```
